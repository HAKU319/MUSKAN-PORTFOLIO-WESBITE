OUR STORY

A craft, carried across a continent.

Studio 57 started, like most beautiful things, with a quiet kind of patience.

Manita came to this country as an immigrant — a young woman in a new city, watching, learning, holding on to the small details that made her feel grounded. The shape of a nail. The weight of a brush. The way a steady hand could turn a hard week into a soft hour.

She picked up the craft early and never put it down. Years of careful work in other people's salons, learning every technique, every product, every kind of hand that needed care — until the day she could finally open a place of her own.

A SPACE OF HER OWN

A small room with big intention.

Studio 57 is that place. Tucked into the heart of Woodside — under the cherry blossoms, behind the glass door — it's a small room with a whole life of practice poured into every appointment.

The wall behind you holds hundreds of shades. The chair you sit in is hers. The hand on yours has been doing this longer than most.

Walk in, or book ahead. Either way, your seat is waiting.

— with love

YOUR SAFETY IS OUR PRIORITY

Hygiene & Sanitation

We adhere to strict hygiene and sanitation protocols to ensure a safe, healthy environment for every client who walks through our door.

Every tool that touches a client gets sterilized after each use. Stations are wiped down between appointments. Disposable liners go in fresh for every pedicure.

It's the part of the work no one sees — but it's the part we never skip.

All tools are thoroughly sterilized after each use, and we use disposable liners for every pedicure.
