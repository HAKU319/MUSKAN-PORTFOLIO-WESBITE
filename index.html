OUR STORY

A craft carried across a continent.

Manita came to this country as an immigrant who started as a young woman in a new city, building her career from the ground up. She spent her early years watching, learning, and observing the small details that make a difference. She picked up the craft from the very beginning and never put it down.

With more than ten years of experience, she worked first in the salons of New York City before moving to Boston to further refine her skills. She spent a decade learning every technique, product, and method of care, building her experience step by step until the day she could finally open a place of her own.

A SPACE OF HER OWN

Built by women, for women.

Today, Studio 57 stands proudly as an independent, women-owned business. Supported and continuously encouraged by her daughters, who contributed their ideas to help bring the dream to life, it has evolved into a welcoming space with a decade of real practice and intention poured into every single appointment.

The wall behind you holds hundreds of shades. The chair you sit in is hers. The hand on yours has been doing this work longer than most.

Walk in, or book ahead. Either way, your seat is waiting.

Hygiene & Sanitation

We adhere to strict hygiene and sanitation protocols to ensure a safe, healthy environment for every client who walks through our door.

Every tool that touches a client gets sterilized after each use. Stations are wiped down between appointments. Disposable liners go in fresh for every pedicure.

It is the core part of our standard practice, meaning it is a fundamental baseline step we never skip.

All tools are thoroughly sterilized after each use, and we use disposable liners for every pedicure.
