01 — The Problem

A cluttered site
was losing donors

The old site buried its donation link three clicks deep behind a cluttered nav. Visitors — many of them older, on mobile — gave up before they gave.

02 — Research

Low contrast,
no accessibility

An audit turned up thin, low-contrast text, no alt text on images, and tap targets too small for older or less tech-confident visitors.

03 — Usability Testing

Five sessions,
one clear pattern

Five usability tests later, the fixes were obvious: bigger tap targets, one clear call to action, and plain language throughout.

04 — Redesign

One path from
story to donation

A simplified nav and accessible layout gave visitors one warm, clear path — no maze required to find the donate button.

05 — Launch

A donate button
that's easy to find

The new homepage leads with the dogs' stories and puts giving front and center — accessible, warm, and unmistakably clear.

06 — Outcome

Donations went up
almost half

Measured over three months of live traffic post-launch: a 42% increase in completed donations.

The result

Every click now feels like it's rooting for you — the way the dogs do.
